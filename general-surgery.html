Appointment

Our Department of General Surgery offers advanced and compassionate surgical care using minimally invasive techniques, state-of-the-art equipment, and patient-centered protocols. We treat a wide spectrum of abdominal, gastrointestinal, endocrine, and soft tissue conditions with precision and care.

Whether it’s an emergency appendectomy, hernia repair, gallbladder removal, or a complex gastrointestinal tumor surgery — our highly skilled surgical team is equipped to deliver the highest standards of surgical care. Our approach blends clinical expertise with modern technology to ensure safe, swift, and effective recovery.

We prioritize early diagnosis, accurate evaluation, and evidence-based surgical interventions. Our operation theatres are equipped with advanced anesthesia monitoring, laparoscopic systems, and infection control measures, enabling us to handle both planned and emergency surgeries seamlessly. Our team is committed to ensuring patients are informed, comfortable, and confident at every step.

We offer both open and laparoscopic (keyhole) surgeries depending on the patient's condition. Minimally invasive surgery leads to less pain, reduced hospital stay, quicker healing, and minimal scarring. Post-operative care is tailored to each patient with diet plans, wound management, and physiotherapy if needed.

Conditions We Treat

Our General Surgery department is proficient in managing a wide range of conditions, including:

  • Appendicitis and Appendectomy
  • Gallstones and Gallbladder Removal
  • Hernia Repair (Inguinal, Umbilical, Incisional)
  • Thyroid and Parathyroid Surgeries
  • Gastrointestinal Bleeding and Ulcers
  • Colorectal and Anal Disorders (Fistula, Fissures, Hemorrhoids)
  • Lumps, Abscesses, and Soft Tissue Tumors
  • Trauma and Emergency Surgical Care

Each patient receives comprehensive pre-operative counseling, detailed diagnosis, and post-operative support. We maintain strict hygiene and sterility to prevent surgical infections and complications.

If you're experiencing persistent abdominal discomfort, swelling, or any condition requiring surgical attention, our expert team is ready to help you heal safely and quickly. Book your consultation today for expert evaluation and care.
